# React Muscle Highlighter

Prime Fitness uses `react-muscle-highlighter` version 1.2.0 by soroojshehryar for its front/back SVG muscle diagrams.

Source: https://github.com/soroojshehryar/react-muscle-highlighter

License: MIT. The accompanying `react-muscle-highlighter-LICENSE.txt` preserves the upstream license notice. Prime Fitness supplies custom colors and maps anatomical surfaces to its existing aggregate muscle groups; those surfaces do not represent independently measured training volume.
