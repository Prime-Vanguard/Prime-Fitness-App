# Prime Fitness self-hosted fonts

Inter, Tajawal, Archivo and Alexandria are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. Their unmodified license notices accompany this file. Latin and Arabic
WOFF2 subsets were obtained from Google Fonts (Inter and Tajawal on November 10,
2026; Archivo and Alexandria on November 28, 2026). The font software has not been
renamed or modified.

- Inter: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter
- Tajawal: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/tajawal
- Archivo: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/archivo
- Alexandria: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/alexandria

The application serves these fonts locally and does not request Google Fonts at
runtime. Build-generated font filenames may contain content hashes.
