# Prime Fitness self-hosted fonts

Inter, Manrope and Tajawal are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.
Their unmodified license notices accompany this file. Latin and Arabic WOFF2
subsets were obtained from Google Fonts on November 10, 2026. The font software
has not been renamed or modified.

- Inter: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/inter
- Manrope: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/manrope
- Tajawal: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/tajawal

The application serves these fonts locally and does not request Google Fonts at
runtime. Build-generated font filenames may contain content hashes.
