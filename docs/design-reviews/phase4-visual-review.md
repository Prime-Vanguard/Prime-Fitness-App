## Intentional source differences

- No invented adherence percentage, target heart rate, recovery diagnosis,
  verified-fasted status, body-fat estimate, Pod bonus or chat encryption claim.
- The overview uses actual completed workouts, current-week workouts and logged
  set-entry counts. Rich strength/volume metrics retain their established scopes.
- The interactive 100-level roadmap and six approved emblems remain; no source
  mockup tier replaces the authoritative ranks or million-XP thresholds.
- Reward images/categories are not fabricated when the existing catalogue has no
  approved image/category fields. Real titles, descriptions, costs and stock drive
  the cards. Fulfillment promises remain the server's existing workflow.
- The follow-up enhancement replaces Community jump links with URL-backed subviews
  that preserve forms. Pod feed remains opted-in workout activity rather than chat
  or an unrestricted roster. `phase4-community-*` captures show Pods;
  `phase4-challenges-*` captures show the separate Challenges view.
- Curated starting points are editorial suggestions, not usage-derived popularity.
- Source viewport/device scaling is unrecorded, and content differs. These captures
  are review material; controlled overlays and user approval are still required
  before claiming exact 1:1 acceptance.

Physical Android/iOS touch, camera, keyboard, installed-PWA and assistive-technology
checks remain pending. No hosted data, permissions or rewards were modified.
